Ray length energy (calm → intense)
Ray angle tempo, slow on the left to fast on the right (60 to 200 BPM)
Tip colour valence: very sad very happy
Tip size popularity (0 to 100)
Roots number and length = acousticness
Ripple rings count = danceability (1 to 5)

How the views connect. Selecting a dot on the mood map opens that genre's burst and draws a dashed link on the map to its nearest neighbour, measured as the shortest distance across standardised danceability, energy, valence and acousticness. The neighbour's burst sits beside it, joined by the same dashed wave from the sketch. Clicking the neighbour moves the focus to it, so you can walk from genre to genre. Search filters straight to a genre by name.
