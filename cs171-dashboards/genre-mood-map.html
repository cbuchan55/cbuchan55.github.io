Valence ranking SKETCH 8

Eight happiest and eight saddest genres. Brush the scatter to rank your own set.

How the views connect. Brushing the scatter filters the ranking to the genres inside the box and averages them in the radar. Hovering a bar or a dot highlights the same genre in the other two views. Clicking pins a genre so you can compare up to three genres on the radar. Radar axes are 0 to 1; loudness (−30 to 0 dB), tempo (50 to 200 BPM) and duration (1 to 7 min) are rescaled to fit.
