Each speaker is a group of songs. Inner cone area = average popularity (0 to 100). Click a speaker to profile it.

Each petal is an energy level; petal length = average danceability. Click a petal to filter the speakers to that level.

Sound profile SKETCH 7

all songs in view selected speaker (orange when it is the loud, not danceable group)

How the views connect. Clicking a petal filters every speaker and the radar to songs at that energy level; click it again, or "Show all energy levels", to clear. Clicking a speaker selects that group: the radar compares it with all songs in view, and the flower dims the petals that contain none of its songs, with a line on each petal showing the selected group's own danceability there. Songs are de-duplicated by track ID (89,741 tracks). Speaker splits are at 0.5. A "hit" is popularity of 50 or more. Energy levels: very low < 0.2, low 0.2 to 0.4, mid 0.4 to 0.6, high 0.6 to 0.8, very high ≥ 0.8.
