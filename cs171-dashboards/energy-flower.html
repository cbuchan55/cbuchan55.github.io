Who fills this petal SKETCH 8

How the views connect. The popularity buttons are a shared filter: the flower and the ranking both recompute from songs in that popularity band, and a grey outline keeps the all-songs flower visible for comparison. Clicking a petal selects an energy level, and the ranking lists the 12 most danceable genres among songs at that level (genres with at least 15 songs there). Hovering a genre draws that genre's own five petals over the flower, so you can see whether it follows the overall shape. Energy levels: very low < 0.2, low 0.2 to 0.4, mid 0.4 to 0.6, high 0.6 to 0.8, very high ≥ 0.8.
